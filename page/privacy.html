Privacy Policy

What we store, why we store it, and the choices you have.

Demo notice

UrbanNest is a demonstration project. Everything below is stored in your own browser (localStorage / sessionStorage) — no personal data is sent to any server.

1. What we store

  • Account details — name, email and password, saved locally when you create an account.
  • Your activity — saved homes, price alerts, and your Lifestyle Match quiz answers.
  • Your session — who is signed in, so the site can personalise what you see.

2. “Keep me signed in”

  • Checked: your session is kept in localStorage, so you stay signed in even after closing the browser.
  • Unchecked: your session is kept in sessionStorage only — it ends automatically when the browser or window closes.
  • Signing out clears the session either way.

3. Contacting agents

  • When you sign in and contact an agent, your name and email would be shared with that agent (demo: no real message is sent).
  • Signed-out visitors cannot contact agents or see contact details.

4. Your choices

  • Sign out from the user menu at any time.
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove everything this demo stores.
  • Read the Terms of Service for the full picture.
